SRMIST's cybersecurity club. Seventeen months, in two roles.

## Software Developer

**Dec 2023 – Oct 2024**

Project work for the club. The main output was [MFAuth](/projects/mfauth/): multi-factor authentication using time-based OTP, built out as a TOTP service, a from-scratch implementation of the algorithm, and a React front end on Firebase auth.

Writing TOTP twice, once with a library and once without, was deliberate. Using Speakeasy teaches you an API. Implementing RFC 6238 by hand teaches you that the whole thing is an HMAC of the clock with four lines of truncation on the end.

## Technical Lead

**Oct 2024 – Apr 2025**

Promoted in October 2024. Ran technical operations and project development, workshops and CTF training, and mentored the juniors coming in.

The training side is the part worth keeping. A club is a pipeline: people arrive knowing nothing and leave able to compete. The bottleneck is never tooling, it's whether anyone sat down with them for the first few challenges.
